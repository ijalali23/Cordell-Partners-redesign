Fee model
Search without the heavy retainer.

We work retained or success-based, whichever suits the mandate. Where a search is unusually complex we propose a modest commitment fee, agreed in writing before work begins.

Approach

Six steps. Every stakeholder met first.

Every search is different, but the discipline is not. We meet the principal, the board and the team before a single candidate call goes out, because culture and fit decide whether a hire lasts.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Working sessions with the principal, stakeholders and the team the hire will lead. We agree the scorecard, compensation range and timeline in writing.

    Week 1
  2. 02

    Map

    A global map of the candidate universe across the GCC, London and other financial centres, shared with you so you see the whole market, not just a shortlist.

    Weeks 1-2
  3. 03

    Approach

    Discreet, partner-led conversations with passive candidates. Your name is disclosed only when you agree it should be.

    Weeks 2-4
  4. 04

    Assess

    Structured competency interviews, referencing and psychometric testing where the role calls for it.

    Weeks 3-5
  5. 05

    Shortlist

    A shortlist with written assessments, presented on a weekly tracking call so you always know where the search stands.

    Weeks 5-6
  6. 06

    Appoint & onboard

    Offer negotiation, notice-period and relocation support, and preparation for DFSA or FSRA approved-individual applications.

    Through day 90
