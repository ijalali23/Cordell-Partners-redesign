Cordell Partners ("we", "us", "our") is an executive search firm based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal data when you use this website or engage our services as a client or candidate.

Who we are

Cordell Partners, The Offices 3, One Central, Dubai, United Arab Emirates. For any privacy question, contact info@cordell-partners.com. Registered entity details: [legal entity name and registration number].

The data we collect

  • Candidates: name, contact details, CV, career history, qualifications, references and any information you choose to share with us.
  • Clients: name, business contact details and the details of the mandates you brief us on.
  • Website visitors: basic technical data such as IP address and browsing activity, where analytics are in use.

How we use it

  • To provide executive search and recruitment services, including matching candidates to mandates.
  • To contact you about roles, mandates or your enquiry.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

Our live mandates are powered by our recruitment platform (Atlas / Recruit with Atlas). Applications submitted through it are handled under that provider's terms in addition to this policy.

Sharing

We share candidate information with clients only with the candidate's knowledge and, where required, consent. We do not sell personal data. We may share data with service providers who support our operations under appropriate confidentiality terms.

Retention

We keep personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above or as required by law. You may ask us to update or delete your data at any time.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, and object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, email info@cordell-partners.com.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be published on this page.
